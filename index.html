CampusBite Kya Hai?

CampusBite ek next-generation digital food-ordering platform hai jise khaas taur par hamare campus ke students, faculty aur staff ke break-time ko aasaan aur fast banane ke liye banaya gaya hai. Aksar lunch break ya classes ke beech 10-15 minute ke gap mein canteen ke bahar lambi bheed lag jati hai, jisse order dene aur khana lene mein hi poora break nikal jata hai. CampusBite is samasya ko khatam karta hai—ab aapke campus ki teeno canteens ka poora menu seedha aapke smartphone par hai.

Simple & Fast

Yeh Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (4 Simple Steps)

1

📲 QR Scan Karein & Website Kholein

Kisi bhi heavy app ko download karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Bas banner par diye gaye QR code ko scan karein aur seedha CampusBite web platform par aayein.

2

🍽️ Apni Canteen Chunein

Campus ki teeno canteens mein se apni pasandida canteen select karein aur unka live menu aur prices check karein.

3

💳 Order Place & UPI Payment

Apne favourite snacks, meals ya beverages cart mein add karein, apni basic details (Name, Course/Department, Pickup Time) bharein aur direct UPI QR ke zariye instant payment karein.

4

⚡ Bina Wait Kiye Pickup Karein

Aapka order seedha canteen counter par chala jata hai. Jaise hi order ready hota hai, counter se apna unique Order Token dikhakar khana collect karein!
